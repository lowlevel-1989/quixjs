# battle-music

Background music in the battle, where the game only has a very quiet
ambience. It is REbelClan, a song that comes with the game, in a loop, below
the sound effects and with the master volume of Options.

It comes off. To turn it on: `battle-music: On` in the Mods option of the
launcher of the port.
