# big-text

Bigger text in the dialogues and the menus. Felvidék is 960x720: on the
640x480 screen of many handhelds everything is shown at two thirds and the
text was too small.

The text of the dialogues, the choices, the menus, the help and the
scrolling text is 25% bigger (24 to 30). The windows keep their size and
place. A long line of dialogue goes on to the next line (the word wrap of
YEP_MessageCore, a plugin of the game) instead of leaving the window.

It comes on. To turn it off: `big-text: Off` in the Mods option of the
launcher of the port.
