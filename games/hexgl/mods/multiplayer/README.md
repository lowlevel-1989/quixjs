# multiplayer

Races between handhelds on the same network (up to 8). The ships of the
others are ghosts: you see them, they do not crash into you.

1. Open HexGL on every handheld and choose **Multiplayer**.
2. In the room you see who is there and if their track is loaded.
3. Anyone presses **A**: everybody starts at the same time.
4. At the end, the times of everybody. **A** goes back to the room.

Start pauses, but the race goes on for the others: **Leave race** goes back
to the room. Whoever raced with the cheat No damage shows `(no damage)` in
the results.

No ports to open and no addresses to type: the handhelds find each other on
the network (the same as Transfer Save).

It comes on. To turn it off: `multiplayer: Off` in the Mods option of the
launcher of the port.
