# volume

Two options in the menu of the port, from 0 to 10: **Music** and
**Effects** (wind, crashes, boost). HexGL plays everything at the same
volume and the music could hardly be heard: by default the music is at 10
and the effects at 5. A change also applies to what is already playing.

It comes on. To turn it off: `volume: Off` in the Mods option of the
launcher of the port.
